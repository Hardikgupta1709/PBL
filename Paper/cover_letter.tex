\begin{letter}{Editor-in-Chief \\ IEEE Conference Proceedings}

\opening{Dear Editor,}

We submit the enclosed manuscript, ``Regime-Adaptive Pairs Trading with
Robust Kalman Filtering: An Integrated Framework with Ablation Analysis
and Live Validation,'' for consideration for publication in your
proceedings.

\textbf{Summary.}
The paper presents a pairs trading framework that couples an adaptive
Kalman filter (with MAD-based outlier robustness) to a random-forest
regime classifier.  The key innovation is that the Kalman filter's
process noise is modulated by the detected market regime, allowing
faster hedge-ratio adaptation during turbulent periods.

\textbf{Evaluation rigour.}
We apply the full battery of modern financial ML evaluation:
expanding walk-forward analysis (32 quarterly folds), 10{,}000-trial
Monte Carlo significance testing, block bootstrap confidence intervals
with Benjamini--Hochberg correction, a 9-configuration ablation study,
7-baseline comparison, 4-source performance attribution, SHAP feature
analysis, rolling cointegration stability analysis, and live paper
trading on Alpaca.

\textbf{Honest results.}
In the spirit of Harvey et al.\ (2016), we report honest negative
out-of-sample results: no tested pair achieves a statistically
significant positive Sharpe ratio after realistic transaction costs.
However, regime gating provides +19.5\% protective alpha on the
strongest pair (BAC/PNC), validating the regime-detection component as
a risk management tool.

\textbf{Reproducibility.}
All code, data pipelines, configuration files, and a Docker container
are provided.  A single command (\texttt{python Research/reproduce.py})
regenerates all 8 tables and 11 figures.

\textbf{Supplementary material} is provided separately and includes
complete hyperparameter tables, extended sensitivity analysis,
per-pair ablation details, walk-forward fold-by-fold results,
full feature importance rankings, and computational cost analysis.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere and all authors have
approved the submission.
